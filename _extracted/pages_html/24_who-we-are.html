We are an independent, non-profit civil organization dedicated to documenting the heritage and history of the Druze Unitarian faith, strengthening Druze cultural identity, and highlighting the contributions of Druze community members across various fields.

We seek to build a trusted platform that provides accurate information, facilitates communication, and presents a balanced and contemporary image of the Druze community to the world. We believe in the importance of knowledge, transparency, and collective action, and we are committed to the principles of neutrality, professionalism, and respect for diversity.
